# Xeta Notifications Organizer Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 3, 2026

Xeta Notifications Organizer uses Android notification access to read notification titles, text, app names, package names, times, icons, and original tap actions. It stores this information in private app storage so you can organize notifications, mark them read, and reopen their original action. Collection can continue while the app is closed while Notification access remains enabled.

The app also reads the names of launcher apps so you can select important and repetitive apps. These selections are stored on this device.

This information is stored only in the app's private storage on your device. The app does not require an account, does not send notification or app selection data to a server, and does not sell or share this data. Saved notification records remain until you delete them in Settings or uninstall the app. App icons and preferences are also kept locally while the app is installed.

You can stop future notification collection at any time by disabling Notification access in Android settings. Use Settings > Delete stored notifications to remove notification records. Uninstalling the app removes its locally stored app data according to Android system behavior.

For privacy questions, contact the developer through the contact address listed on the Google Play store page.
